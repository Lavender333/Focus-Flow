Privacy Policy

Focus Flow is designed as a private relaxation and focus experience. The app does not require a Focus Flow account.

Data

Your preferences, saved rituals, and garden entries are stored locally on your device or browser. They are not sent to a Focus Flow server.

Purchases

Studio purchases and restore checks are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. Focus Flow uses purchase status only to unlock Studio access. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers and related transaction information according to their own policies.

Audio

Microphone and uploaded audio tools run locally for practice feedback. Focus Flow does not upload or store recordings on a server.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the Focus Flow GitHub repository.
